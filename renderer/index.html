Printer calibration

One-time check: prints a ruler/crosshair pattern through the same pipeline as a real job, so you can measure any offset or cropping against the printed paper.

Measure a 20 mm interval on the printed calibration sheet and enter it here. If it reads longer than 20, the printer is enlarging the image and cropping the edges; every print is then pre-shrunk by that factor to cancel it out. Leave at 20 for no correction.
